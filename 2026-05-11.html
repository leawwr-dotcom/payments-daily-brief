Visa Scales Agentic Commerce via Stripe SPT — Card Rails Plug Into Agents

Visa expands Stripe Payment Token support to Visa Intelligent Commerce + Mastercard Agent Pay, plus Affirm / Klarna BNPL for agents.
Agentic AI
💡 BD Angle: Note what just happened: Stripe SPT — originally Stripe's tokenization play — is now the connective tissue between Visa's network-issued agent tokens, Mastercard Agent Pay, AND Affirm/Klarna BNPL inside agent flows. Card networks aren't getting disrupted by agentic commerce; they're issuing the credentials and writing the protocols (AP2 mandates, accepted by Mastercard as Verifiable Intent, signed onto by Visa + Stripe). For BD: stop pitching "stablecoin will replace cards in agent commerce." It's a both/and. The card networks have moved fast enough that the realistic 2026-27 architecture is dual-rail — USDC for machine-to-machine + low-value/cross-border, card-network agent tokens for consumer + high-trust merchants. Position your stack as rail-agnostic.
