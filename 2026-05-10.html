Exodus Launches XO Cash — First Stablecoin Built for AI Agents

NYSE-listed Exodus ships XO Cash on Solana with AgentKit SDK; every agent wallet gets a Visa debit card via one API call.
Stablecoin × Agentic AI
💡 BD Angle: The agent-payment stack is fragmenting fast: AWS x402 (developer/API), Stripe Issuing for Agents (enterprise), now Exodus XO Cash (consumer). Exodus's wedge: keys never leave the platform, users set hard rules (daily caps, allowed merchants, rate limits) per agent. For SEA prospects building agent-driven commerce, the pitch is no longer "do you support agent payments" — it's "which agent-payment SDK do we standardize on, and where does our VASP/license layer plug in?" Position your KYT and compliance stack as SDK-agnostic.
🎯 Cross-border SEA🎯 Agentic Stack
🇨🇳 中文速读
纽交所上市的 Exodus 推出 XO Cash——首个专为 AI 代理设计的稳定币，基于 Solana，配套 AgentKit SDK。每个代理钱包可通过一次 API 调用申请 Visa 借记卡，结账时自动兑换成 USDC 或 USDT，无手续费。用户可对每个代理设置每日额度、单笔上限、可用商户、调用频率等硬性规则。
💡 商业洞察：代理支付协议栈快速分化：AWS x402（开发者侧）、Stripe Issuing for Agents（企业侧）、Exodus XO Cash（消费者侧）。东南亚销售对话的关键问题已从"是否支持代理支付"切换为"接入哪个 SDK，以及合规/KYT 中间件如何挂载"——把自家产品定位为 SDK 中立。
Sources: GlobeNewswire, Crypto Briefing, Stock Titan

HK's RedotPay Plugs Stripe-Paradigm Tempo Chain Into 7M-User Stablecoin Network

RedotPay integrates Tempo's Machine Payments Protocol (MPP); first major consumer agentic-payments distribution win for the Stripe/Paradigm-incubated L1.
Stablecoin × Agentic AI × SEA Pulse
💡 BD Angle: Two reads. (1) Tempo is the chain Stripe is quietly betting on for the agent economy — and RedotPay (HK-based, 7M users across 100+ countries with strong SEA penetration) just gave it the first real consumer distribution. Tempo vs. Solana is now the chain-layer fight to watch. (2) For SEA conversations: RedotPay-on-Tempo is the closest analog yet to "WeChat Pay for AI agents in Asia." If you're pitching cross-border PSP infra into PH/ID/VN, expect "how does this compare to RedotPay's agent rail?" within the next 60–90 days. The China–HK–SEA stablecoin corridor is consolidating — fast.
🎯 Cross-border SEA🎯 China–HK–SEA🎯 Tempo Chain
🇨🇳 中文速读
总部在香港的 RedotPay（覆盖 100+ 国家、700 万用户）接入 Tempo 的 Machine Payments Protocol（MPP）。Tempo 是由 Stripe 与 Paradigm 共同孵化的 Layer-1 公链，专为支付场景设计。整合后，AI 代理可通过 RedotPay 用稳定币完成从商品搜索到下单结算的全自动闭环。
💡 商业洞察：两条线索：(1) 公链层之争——Tempo vs Solana 谁先抢下代理支付主战场，RedotPay 让 Tempo 拿到首个大规模消费端落地。(2) 中国—香港—东南亚稳定币走廊正在加速整合，未来 60–90 天东南亚 PSP 销售对话中"对比 RedotPay 代理通道"将成为标准问题。
Sources: GlobeNewswire, Blockchain Reporter, Manila Times

Stablecoin Card Spend +105% YoY; Rain Joins Mastercard at $1.95B

Rain becomes Mastercard Principal Member (was Visa-only); retail stablecoin card spend up 105% YoY but still <1% of global volume; on-chain settlement saves 40% trapped capital.
Stablecoin
💡 BD Angle: Stablecoin cards are now bi-network — Visa AND Mastercard. The hard data point you can use in any PSP/issuer conversation: on-chain settlement reduces trapped capital by 40% because cards settle 24/7 (no weekends, no holidays). That's a CFO-grade number, not an evangelism pitch. Pair this with WU's USDPT launch (yesterday's brief) — the institutional stablecoin-card thesis just got a clear ROI metric. For SEA: SGD/PHP/IDR card programs running on legacy T+1/T+2 cycles are now quantifiably uncompetitive for cross-border treasury. Lead with the 40% number.
🎯 Card Settlement🎯 Cross-border SEA
🇨🇳 中文速读
稳定币初创 Rain（估值 19.5 亿美元）成为 Mastercard 主要会员（Principal Member），从此前的 Visa 独家扩展为双卡组织覆盖。Rain 高管在 Consensus Miami 2026 披露：过去一年零售稳定币卡支出同比增长约 105%，但仍占全球卡支出不到 1%；链上结算让卡项目在周末/节假日仍可清算，资金占用减少 40%。
💡 商业洞察："链上结算节省 40% 占压资金"是 CFO 级别的硬指标，远比"稳定币是趋势"更有销售杀伤力。东南亚 SGD/PHP/IDR 卡项目仍跑 T+1/T+2 传统清算——把这条数字直接放进开场白，而不是埋在第三页 PPT。
Sources: CoinDesk, Fortune, Crypto Briefing

Stripe Sessions 2026: Agentic Commerce Suite + UCP Lock In Google Gemini

Stripe ships 288 launches; Agentic Commerce Suite + Universal Commerce Protocol now power checkout in Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot, Meta — Etsy, URBN, Coach onboarded.
Agentic AI
💡 BD Angle: Stripe just won the agent-commerce OS war for Western retail. UCP is open-standard but Stripe controls the reference implementation — and a single integration now exposes merchants to four major AI surfaces (Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot, Meta). For SEA: this is the "default" SEA merchants will demand from local PSPs within 6–12 months. Pitch local PSPs/VASPs on UCP-readiness — those that can't quickly support the protocol risk losing mid-market merchants to Stripe direct. For local marketplaces (Shopee/Lazada/Tokopedia), the strategic question is whether to integrate UCP, fork it, or build a competitor.
🎯 Cross-border SEA🎯 Agentic Stack
🇨🇳 中文速读
Stripe Sessions 2026 推出 288 项更新，核心是 Agentic Commerce Suite——一次集成即可在 Gemini、ChatGPT、Copilot、Meta 四大 AI 入口完成销售；并联合 Google 推出开放标准 Universal Commerce Protocol（UCP），首批接入品牌包括 Etsy、Coach、Kate Spade、Urban Outfitters。
💡 商业洞察：Stripe 实质上拿下了西方零售的"代理商务操作系统"。东南亚 PSP/VASP 销售切入点——把"UCP-ready"作为产品差异化卖点，6–12 个月内中型商户会主动询问。Shopee、Lazada、Tokopedia 必须三选一：接入、分叉、还是自建。
