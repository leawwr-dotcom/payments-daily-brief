Visa Plugs Card Rails Into Stripe's Machine Payments Protocol — Anchor Validator on Tempo

Visa shipped a card-based MPP spec and SDK, letting merchants/PSPs/acquirers accept card payments inside Stripe-Tempo agentic flows. Visa is also now an "anchor validator" on Tempo alongside Stripe and Zodia Custody.
Agentic AI · Card Networks
💡 BD Angle: A week ago the agentic-payments narrative was "card networks vs. stablecoins." This week it's "card networks and stablecoins, both via Stripe-Tempo." For any merchant or acquirer prospect in SEA, the qualifying question is no longer "do you want to support agent payments?" — it's "do you want to support agents on cards, stablecoins, or both, and which PSP gives you all three in one integration?" That last clause is the wedge: most regional PSPs offer one rail; the ones who offer card + stablecoin + agent-routing in a single API win 2026. For MetaComp specifically: AgentX positioning should emphasise "stack-agnostic" — agents should be able to settle on whichever rail is cheapest/fastest per transaction, not be locked to one network's framework.

SEA Pulse · Corridor
